Super Samus World: Fallen Mushroom Kingdom — v0.5.0 Alpha

The Mushroom Kingdom has fallen silent. Something dangerous now walks its familiar paths—and Samus is stepping into the ruins.

Explore a darker take on a familiar world, with rebuilt character animations, a new soundtrack, distinct level palettes, and enemies that do more than wait to be shot. Expect armored opponents, relentless pursuers, tactical firefights, and explosive combat.

Already implemented:
- Expanded beam, missile, and Morph Ball combat.
- Redesigned enemies with new animations, attacks, and pursuit behavior.
- Missile splash damage and destructible-block interactions.
- Updated player animations, wall jumps, and Morph Ball traversal.
- Custom music, sound effects, and separate music/effects volume controls.
- Normal level progression and numerous gameplay and visual fixes.
This is an actively developed alpha. Bugs, rough edges, and balance changes are expected. Player feedback will help shape future versions.

Windows PC. Free to play. Requires player-supplied Super Mario World and Super Metroid ROMs during first-time setup. No ROMs are included.
